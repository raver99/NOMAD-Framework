## Knowledge Base

What this project holds to be true lives in `KNOWLEDGE.md` at the project root and the documents it
indexes. Its header states where documents go, how they are named and what shape they take; follow
that rather than a convention carried in from elsewhere.

Every external claim in a document carries a numbered reference with its tier and the date it was
retrieved. Values on someone else's release cycle — versions, deadlines, prices — are not recorded;
the document records the gate and links the source that states the current value.

Research produces a dated record under `docs/research/`. A knowledge base document is what survived
review of that record. The record is frozen evidence; the document is maintained.
